المرحلة 2: محرك اللحظات المفصلية (Feature & Event Engineering)
بدلاً من تشغيل الـ 5 دقائق كفيديو خام فقط، يبحث الكود في جدول الأحداث لاستخراج الأحداث ذات القيمة التكتيكية العالية:

التسديدات (Shots): مكان التسديد، المسافة للمرمى، ومسار الكرة.

التمريرات التقدمية (Packing / Progressive Passes): التمريرات التي تكسر خطوط الخصم وتتجاوز عدداً من مدافعيه (يتم حساب عدد المدافعين المتجاوزين مباشرة من إحداثيات التتبع في نفس الفريم).

العرضيات والكرات الثابتة (Crosses & Set Pieces): الكرات الهوائية المؤثرة داخل الـ 18.

التدخلات والقطع (Interceptions & Tackles): استرجاع الكرة تحت الضغط.

المرحلة 3: التوليد السردي بالذكاء الاصطناعي (Multi-Voice Storytelling)
ربط أرقام كل حدث مهم بموديل توليد نصوص ذكي لإنشاء 3 نبرات تعليق تلفزيوني (عربي وإنجليزي):

نبرة المحلل التكتيكي (Analyst): يركز على المساحات، الضغط العكسي، وسحب المدافعين.

نبرة المشجع (Casual Fan): تعليق حماسي على سرعة الهجمة والخطورة.

نبرة اللاعب الفردي (Player Focus): تقييم قرار اللاعب وزاوية رؤيته للملعب.

المرحلة 4: الواجهة النهائية (Full Broadcast Experience)
التحكم في سرعة الفيديو وزمن كل لقطة (إبطاء السرعة تلقائياً عند اقتراب لحظة التسديد أو التمرير الحاسم).

تقسيم الشاشة: ثلثين للملعب التكتيكي والثلث لبطاقات السرد والإحصائيات اللحظية (Distance, Velocity, Bypassed Defenders).

Football stories do not start and end with the scoreline. In this hackathon, you will build AIdriven applications and agents that help transform synthetic football events into explainable match intelligence. Projects should help surface meaningful stats, identify key moments, explain why they matter, and generate personalized experiences. Projects must use synthetic, football-realistic data. No real Premier League match data is redistributed to participants. Participants should deliver an AI-powered solution that transforms synthetic football events into explainable match intelligence, real-time narratives, and automated match recaps suitable for studio, broadcast, or streaming scenarios, and flexible enough for fans in any market to tailor their experience.

Your Project may take the form of an AI agent, workflow, integration, data platform, analytics experience, automation solution, or an entirely new experience. Your Project should fit seamlessly into the intended use cases, be responsive enough for live play, clear enough for real-time narratives, and automated match recaps suitable for studio, broadcast, or streaming scenarios and flexible enough for fans in any market to tailor their experience.

(i) One pipeline with five stages: Your Project should:

(a) Ingest synthetic match events as they happen (passes, shots, tackles, possession changes, pressure events, match metadata, etc.).

(b) Interpret them into meaningful football statistics, patterns, and the context behind them.

(c) Explain why a moment matters rather than only reporting that it happened.

(d) Render that insight into something that can sit on screen alongside the match.

(e) Personalize it so the same intelligence reaches a data-hungry analyst and a casual fan as two genuinely different experiences. Your Project should make every match feel made for every fan, including customized modes, favorite club(s), favorite player(s), player focused mode to focus on the player throughout the match and welcome many more ideas. Your Project might focus on on-screen storytelling, clean outputs, timed and machine-readable enough to be rendered as synchronized graphical overlays on a live match feed, in the way the platform's rendering partner handles overlays in the production path. Or your Project might focus on personalized audience experiences, viewing modes shaped by language preference, favorite club, favorite player, or a single performance metric a viewer cares about. Or it could do both, a Project that only generates metrics has no audience, and a solution that only personalizes has nothing to personalize.

(ii) Real-time match intelligence: ingest synthetic match events and aggregate them into meaningful football states and statistics. Features you should consider incorporating into your Project include:

Player identification: real-time player name tagging, with optional speed and distance indicators triggered when predefined thresholds are reached

Pass quality: pass distance, pass accuracy and a pass difficulty rating

Speed metrics: ball speed and shot speed

Auto-eventing: running a model over an incoming video feed to generate the pass and speed metrics above automatically, rather than tagging them by hand

(iii) Narrative generation: go beyond metrics to produce key narratives, player-performance context, notable milestones, and match recaps.

(iv) Explainability: explain why moments matter, such as control vs. chaos, tactical pressure changes, and game rhythm patterns.

(v) Data innovation: create or extend synthetic football-realistic datasets with events such as passes, shots, tackles, possession changes, pressure events, and match metadata.

(vi) Multi-language storytelling: generate clear natural-language outputs that could support global studio and streaming scenarios in multiple languages.
